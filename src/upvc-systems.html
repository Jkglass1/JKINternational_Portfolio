Engineering Strengths

Designed For Extreme Indian Weather & Lifelong Performance

Our uPVC door and window systems provide proven advantages over traditional timber and untreated materials, backed by rigorous factory fabrication.

Multi-Chamber Profiles

Internal multi-cavity chambers that break thermal conduction and reduce outside sound penetration by up to 30-40 dB when combined with DGU glass.

Steel Reinforcement

Hot-dip galvanized internal steel cores inserted into every critical profile section to ensure structural wind-load strength and prevent warping.

Weather & UV Resistance

Tropical-grade titanium dioxide formulation protecting profiles from yellowing, chalking, or embrittlement under intense tropical UV exposure.

Zero Maintenance

Termite-proof, rot-free, non-corrosive material that never requires sanding or repainting. Cleans effortlessly with soap and water.
