Toughened Glass

Heat-treated for 4–5× the strength of annealed glass. Shatters into small, harmless granules — ideal for façades, balustrades and shower enclosures.

Request a Quote Chat on WhatsApp
Toughened glass is a high-strength safety glass that undergoes an advanced thermal heat-treatment process designed to create strong compressive stresses on its surface. This specialized treatment significantly improves its durability, making it far more resistant to impact than ordinary glass. Widely used in modern architecture, tempered glass delivers exceptional strength, clarity, and safety performance. Its robust nature makes it ideal for areas that face frequent mechanical stress, heavy foot traffic, or continuous usage. The glass is also engineered to withstand substantial thermal variations, minimizing the risk of breakage caused by sudden temperature changes. With superior load-bearing capacity, it maintains structural stability even under demanding conditions. In the event of breakage, toughened glass shatters into small, blunt fragments—greatly reducing the chances of injury. Because of these advantages, it has become a preferred material for structural, architectural, and decorative applications. It is commonly used in façades, doors, partitions, shower enclosures, railings, and high-performance glazing systems. Additionally, toughened glass offers long service life, excellent optical clarity, and a refined aesthetic appeal, making it suitable for modern design requirements. Its combination of strength, safety, and visual elegance continues to make it one of the most reliable choices in today’s building and interior industries.

Best Quality toughened glass is made by using latest technique of forced convection type furnace.

Characteristics

Strength
Toughened glass is 4-5 times stronger than equivalent thickness of annealed float glass.
High Thermal Shock Strength
Toughened glass provides greater thermal strength.
Safety
Toughened glass is extremely difficult to break, but if it does, it shatters into small, harmless fragments—eliminating dangerous sharp edges and greatly reducing injury risks.
