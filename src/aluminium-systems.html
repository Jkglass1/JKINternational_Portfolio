Engineered aluminium architectural systems combining structural rigidity, slim sightlines, and smooth operation. From high-span sliding patio doors to commercial partitions and curtain wall framework, fabricated in-house to exact specifications.

Fabrication Capabilities

Engineered For Performance, Durability & Aesthetics

Combining precision multi-angle mitering, hydraulic corner crimping, and integrated glass-fitting with our in-house glass division for single-source reliability.

Slimline Sightlines

Minimalist structural profile geometries engineered to maximize natural daylight while maintaining high wind-load deflection resistance.

Heavy-Duty Hardware

Stainless steel multi-point locking gear, silent tandem rollers, and friction stays engineered for thousands of smooth operating cycles.

Weather & Acoustic Sealing

EPDM double/triple gasket barriers and brush seals engineered to resist driving rainwater, air infiltration, and ambient city noise.

Durable Finishes

Offered in high-micron architectural anodized, premium polyester powder coating, and wooden sublimation textures for long exterior life.
