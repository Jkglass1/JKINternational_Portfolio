We Offer the Best Quality

Quality wise, J. K. Glasses has always been declared victorious because we pay special attention to the quality norms at every stage of production. Our hi-tech in-house quality inspection department is outfitted with every necessary testing equipments. We are an ISO 9001:2000 certified company which itself is a major testimony of our quality production. Our highly experienced quality personnel put their heart and soul while discharging their duties.

We follow stringent quality control measures, right from the stage procurement of raw materials to the stage of final delivery of goods, to ensure that our customers get the best that they deserve.

Our Quality Commitment

“We, at J.K.Glasses are committed in maximizing customer satisfaction, hence delivering cost effective products meeting international standards within time limits by using innovative and environmental friendly technologies for higher levels of productivity and efficiency through continual improvement in Quality Management Systems”.
