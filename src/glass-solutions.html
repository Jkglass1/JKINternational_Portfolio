Glass Processing & Architectural Solutions

From precision thermal tempering and PVB lamination to curved and acoustic insulating glass, JK International processes superior quality architectural and security glass with over 20 years of manufacturing mastery.

Processing Capabilities

State-of-the-Art Glass Processing Equipment

Our manufacturing plants in Kandrori (H.P.) and Jalandhar (Punjab) are equipped with world-class forced convection furnace lines, automated CNC cutting tables, and clean-room lamination lines.

Thermal Toughening

Forced convection horizontal tempering furnaces inducing uniform compressive surface stress for 4-5x annealed strength.

PVB Lamination

De-airing autoclaves and clean-room assembly for multi-layer safety, acoustic containment, and bullet-resistant specifications.

Insulating / DGU

Dual-sealed automated secondary and primary silicone/polyisobutylene sealant lines with molecular sieve desiccant spacers.

Precision Edging & CNC

High-speed flat polish, miter, bevelling, waterjet cutouts, and hole drilling ready for structural spider & patch fittings.
